Trek Readiness

How close is real-world technology to Star Trek's? Each technology is rated one capability at a time, because a tricorder-like sensor can be on sale while tricorder-like diagnosis is still a lab project.

How to read the ratings

Shipping
A consumer can buy it today.
Prototype
A working version exists, but a consumer cannot buy it. This includes lab, military, professional-medical and spaceflight hardware.
Research
Theory or lab results exist, but no working system does the job.
Fiction
Nothing like it works outside stories.
