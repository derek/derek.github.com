Resort Row

New game

Buy land beside the road, get building permits from the zoning board, and add entrances. When a rival stops at one of your entrances, they roll for how many nights they stay and pay your nightly rate.

Game log

    How to play Resort Row

    Each player starts with $15,000. Take turns rolling one die and moving clockwise around the road. Every time you pass or land on the Bank, collect $2,000.

    Where you land

    • Land (green): buy the vacant lot beside this space.
    • Build (orange): apply for a building permit on any lot you own.
    • ? (purple): draw a lucky break card. Some are good, some are not.
    • City Hall: place one free entrance on a resort you have built.
    • Land Office: buy any vacant lot on the board.
    • Tourism Board: collect $200 for each entrance you own.
    • Landing on an open road space next to your own built resort lets you add an entrance there.

    Building permits

    Pick a lot and how many stages to build, in order. Then roll the zoning die:

    • Approved (3 faces): pay the normal price and build.
    • Half price: pay half and build.
    • Double price: pay double, or turn it down.
    • Rejected: nothing is built this turn.

    Each stage raises the resort's nightly rate. A resort's first building comes with one free entrance. After you build, you may buy more entrances on any open road space along that lot.

    Guests

    If you land on a rival's entrance, roll the die. That is how many nights you stay. Pay nights × the resort's nightly rate to the owner.

    Running out of money

    If you cannot pay, your buildings are sold back to the bank for half price, starting with your cheapest resort, then your land. If that is still not enough, you are out and your lots go back on the market.

    Winning

    Be the last player standing, or have the highest net worth (cash, plus what you paid for land, buildings and entrances) when the last round ends.
